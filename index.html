    Lead time on a storm forecast: load shedding before the incident. Prevented, not detected.

    The problem

    Factories rely on scattered tools and isolated threshold alarms. Operators drown in alarm floods and false positives, with no explanation of why an alert fired, while real dangers emerge from combinations of signals no single sensor sees.

    The team

    Firas BennaniAmal MecherguiChaima KidissiMohamed Allagui

    The solution

    • Explainable confidence. Noisy-OR fusion of independent evidence with per-sensor trust and a capped severity matrix: how confident, how severe, which sources.
    • Human-in-the-loop. Stop machine, load shedding, setpoint reduction only run after owner approval in n8n; the incident closes only once the risk is verified to be falling.
    • Predictive. An 82% storm forecast triggers preventive actions and the incident is marked PREVENTED.
    • Cyber agent. Detects attacks (MITRE ATT&CK for ICS) and rejects forged HMAC-signed messages between agents.
    • Live 3D digital twin with full event history and traceability.
